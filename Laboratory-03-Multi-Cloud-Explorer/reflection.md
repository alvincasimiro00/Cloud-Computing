 Reflection

 1. Which cloud provider impressed you the most?
>AWS impressed me the most because it is the biggest and has the most services available. I also like that it has a Free Tier so students like me can learn without paying. But GCP also impressed me because it runs on Google's own fast network — that's really amazing.

 2. What similarities did you see among AWS, Azure, and GCP?
>I noticed that all three are actually very similar. They all have virtual machines, storage, databases, and networking. They all work the same way — you pay for what you use. Once you learn one, you can easily understand the others because the concepts are the same, just different names.

 3. How do business needs affect choosing a cloud platform?
>I learned that you should not choose based only on which one you like. You must choose based on what the business actually needs. If they use Microsoft already → choose Azure. If they do AI → choose GCP. If they are a startup → choose AWS. The business needs should always come first.

 4. What did you learn as a Cloud Solutions Consultant?
>I learned that being a consultant means listening first. You cannot just recommend your favorite platform. You need to understand the client's situation — their budget, their existing systems, and their goals. The best solution is the one that solves their problem simply and affordably.

 5. How has your GitHub portfolio improved?
>Now my portfolio shows more than just steps. It shows research, comparison, and recommendations. Anyone can see how I think and decide. It looks professional now and shows that I can research and advise — which is a very useful skill.
